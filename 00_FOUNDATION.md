The blog's commentary contains factual errors (dating of the Art of War, the Musashi "wooden two-handed style" garble, Frederick "succeeding" de Saxe, the Mahan title). Each corpus file corrects its own entry; the list is the foundation, the commentary is not.

## Brief for the book

A fundamental book of strategy for a man in the modern world, written as if he had just entered it and had nothing. Every chapter must be traceable to what these texts actually say, and every translation from war to the modern life must show its reasoning. Claims that fail verification are cut, not softened.
